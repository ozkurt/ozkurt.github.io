I am fascinated by microbiomes across diverse hosts, and my research asks how microbial communities assemble, persist and are passed on, from wheat seeds to the human gut across the life course.

I currently work in the Human Studies Team at the [Quadram Institute](https://quadram.ac.uk/people/ezgi-ozkurt/) in Norwich, UK, where I led the **PEARL-AGE** family cohort study on the transmission of gut microbes across generations. My work combines wet-lab method development, such as enriching the rare microbial biosphere in faecal samples, with large-scale computational analysis of public gut metagenomes to identify age-related bacterial guilds. I also develop open tools for microbiome data analysis, including [LotuS2](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-022-01365-1) for amplicon sequencing.

I will soon join **Boğaziçi University**, Institute of Environmental Sciences, as an Assistant Professor, supported by a TÜBİTAK 2232-A fellowship. In my fellowship project, we will test whether beneficial microbial partnerships lost during wheat domestication can be restored in modern wheat, and how this affects plant health and adaptation. To do this, we will isolate seed-borne microbes from wild wheat relatives and assemble them into synthetic microbial communities (SynComs) for colonization experiments of domesticated wheat. For more information, see the [project page](/projects/1_project/). 
Beyond wheat, I plan to study microbiomes across different hosts with a focus on microbial ecology and the dynamics of community assembly.

I am building a new research group and am always happy to hear from people interested in microbiomes, whether for collaborations, joining a project, a summer research project, writing grant applications together, or any other idea you may have. Please see the [open positions](/positions/) or [get in touch](mailto:ezgi.ozkurt@quadram.ac.uk). 

**Interests:** microbial ecology · community assembly · vertical transmission · seed microbiomes · domestication and the microbiome · synthetic communities (SynComs) · numerical ecology · microbiome methods & teaching
