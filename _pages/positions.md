<p class="op-lead">
I am building a new research group at the <strong>Institute of Environmental Sciences, Boğaziçi University</strong> (Istanbul), studying how microbial communities assemble, persist and are passed on in diverse hosts. We combine wet-lab experiments with microbiome data analysis, particularly numerical ecology and microbial genome analysis. 
I aim to build a diverse team, in terms of both personal background and scientific experience, to tackle challenging questions with implications for both basic science and applications. I value intellectual curiosity, open-minded and progressive thinking, and collaborative, shared problem-solving, and I aim to create a supportive and inclusive environment for everyone in the group.
</p>

### 4 funded PhD positions in plant microbiomes <span class="op-badge op-open">Open</span>

<div class="op-meta">
  <span><i class="fa-solid fa-hourglass-half"></i> Apply by: open until filled</span>
  <span><i class="fa-solid fa-calendar"></i> Start: 2027 (month to be discussed)</span>
  <span><i class="fa-solid fa-clock"></i> Funding duration: 3 years (possible extension)</span>
  <span><i class="fa-solid fa-coins"></i> Funding: TÜBİTAK 2232-A</span>
</div>

These positions are funded by TÜBİTAK as part of the [Rewilding seed microbiomes](/projects/1_project/) project. We will test whether seed-borne microbes from wild wheat relatives can colonise modern wheat, and how they affect plant growth and the plant's own microbiome.

**You will:**

- Design axenic (sterile) plant experiments
- Profile seed-borne microbial communities in wheat
- Build bacterial culture collections from plant material
- Design SynComs for colonisation experiments
- Analyse amplicon and metagenomic data

**You bring:**

- A BSc and/or MSc in Biology, Molecular Biology & Genetics, Agricultural Engineering or a related field is an advantage, but no particular background is required: if you come from a different field, we will help you get the cross-training you need.
- Some research experience, such as a thesis, time in a lab, or some experience with coding. It does not need to overlap with our work.
- Above all, a genuine interest in the topic, motivation, and a creative mind that is open to learning.

**MSc students:** these positions are intended for PhD students. In exceptional cases, we may consider an MSc student who already has research experience beyond coursework.

**Admission:** a project position is separate from university admission. You also need to be admitted to the Institute of Environmental Sciences at Boğaziçi University, which has its own calendar and requirements, so the two applications run in parallel ([graduate admissions](https://adaylar.bogazici.edu.tr/en-EN/Page/Admissions/Graduate/All)). If you are interested, please get in touch as early as possible, so we can plan your application and start date together.

</div>

<h2 id="other-routes" class="op-section">Other ways to join</h2>

I welcome enquiries from people at every career stage who would like to apply for their own funding to join the group. Get in touch early, as most schemes need a host letter and time to prepare a strong application.

<div class="op-grid">

<div class="op-card" markdown="1">

### Postdocs

At this level, I look for proactive researchers who take ownership of their project, develop their own ideas within the group's research, and help mentor students. In return, I will support you in building your independent research profile, including fellowship and grant applications.

Fellowship schemes to consider:

- TÜBİTAK 2218
- Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowships
- EMBO Postdoctoral Fellowships

</div>

<div class="op-card" markdown="1">

### PhD students

At this level, I look for motivated, curious students who are eager to learn new skills and ready to gradually take ownership of their project. I will provide close supervision and training, and support you in presenting your work, building collaborations and developing your own independent research path.
Admission is through the [Institute of Environmental Sciences graduate programme](#), so please also check its deadlines and requirements.

Funding to consider:

- TÜBİTAK 2211
- YÖK 100/2000

</div>

<div class="op-card" markdown="1">

Funding to consider:

- TÜBİTAK 2210

<h2 id="who" class="op-section">Who I am looking for</h2>

- Interest in wet-lab work, data analysis, or ideally both, and a willingness to learn
